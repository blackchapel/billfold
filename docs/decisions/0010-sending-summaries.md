# 0010 Sending summaries

**Context.** Phase 6 emails a month's summary, with the bill PDFs attached, to the people who need it. It is manual (nobody is emailed unless a person presses the button), free, and must never send something different from what was previewed.

**Decisions.**
- **One email per recipient.** A `sends` row is one press of the button; a `send_items` row is one recipient. Nobody sees anyone else's address, each delivery has its own status, error and retries, and one refused address cannot block the others.
  Limits: 25 recipients, 10 sends per person per hour (the Postgres rate limiter, ADR 0004), 200 people in a list.
- **Finished text is stored.** `sends.subject`, `body_html`, `body_text` and the list of attached `bill_files` are saved when the send starts. Retrying a week later sends exactly what was previewed, and the sent log shows the email as it went out (in a sandboxed iframe, no scripts).
  The preview uses the same `SendComposer` as the real send, so they cannot drift apart.
- **Processing mirrors runs.** The progress page calls a server action that claims one waiting recipient (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, abandoned after 5 minutes), downloads the PDFs from Drive, sends, and records the result. Pause, cancel and retry work like they do for runs. A failed email is retried up to 3 times; the error shown is a fixed sentence, provider text is logged only after redaction.
- **Attachments are fetched at send time** from the Drive connection that holds each file (the app stores no files). One PDF per bill, at most 40 and 18 MB in total (mail servers refuse about 25 MB once encoded). If the limit is exceeded nothing is attached and the preview says so; the bills still list Drive links.
- **Sender.** A Gmail connection (project B, `gmail.send` scope, raw MIME posted to the Gmail API) or an SMTP app-password connection (nodemailer). The sender address is the connected account; the display name is the person who pressed Send.
- **`{{sender}}` and `{{from_name}}`.** `{{sender}}` is the person who pressed Send. `{{from_name}}` is the optional "Name on this account" typed on a Gmail or SMTP connection (stored in its non-secret settings as `senderName`; Billfold cannot read the account owner's name because it asks Google only for the email address). Without a name it falls back to the sender, so a sign-off is never blank. The Send preview passes the chosen account so it shows the same text the send will store.
- **Templates.** Built-in "Standard summary" always exists; an organisation may add named templates (subject, opening text, closing text) and pick a default. Only a fixed list of `{{placeholders}}` is allowed, checked when saving, replaced by plain find-and-replace. The table of bills and the attachment list are generated by Billfold, not by the template.
- **Safety.** Every value from a person or from a bill is HTML-escaped; subjects have line breaks removed so nothing can inject a header; recipient names with control characters are refused; addresses are validated and stored lower-case; the preview and the sent view render in `<iframe sandbox>`.
- **Permissions.** `reports.send` (owner/admin by default, grantable per member), `recipients.manage`, `templates.manage`. Everyone in the organisation can read the lists and the sent log.
- **Test send.** "Send me a test" sends only to the signed-in person, with `[Test]` in the subject, and is recorded in the log as a test.

**Consequences.** Real Gmail/SMTP delivery and real Drive downloads cannot be exercised in the sandbox beyond a local SMTP server and mocked HTTP; the owner tests a send on billfold-dev with a real account. Scheduled sending stays out of scope. A bounce that happens after the mail server accepted the message is not visible to Billfold.
